Delivery Checker

Paste your NSE data to see whether real buyers or sellers are behind the price. "Delivery" means shares that were taken home, not sold again the same day.

How it works
  • A green day closed above its open. A red day closed at or below it.
  • All averages count busy days more than quiet days: total shares delivered divided by total shares traded. "Delivered per day" is the average number of delivered shares per day.
  • Buyers or sellers "in control" means one colour of day delivered at least 20% more shares per day than the other.
  • "Normal" is the average of the 20 days before that day (at least 5 are needed). Strong, Medium and Weak compare a day with normal. Days with very few shares traded are marked down.
  • "Looks OK to go long" needs all 8 checks to pass: above the trendline, green candle, closed near the top, delivery 1.2 times normal, trading 1.5 times normal, recent closes strong, green days delivering at least as much as red days, and few selling days (1 allowed per 5 days).

To use it like an app: Android Chrome menu, then "Install app". iPhone Safari: Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Your data stays on your device.
