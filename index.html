Delivery Checker

Find out whether real investors are behind a price move. Delivery % is the share of traded shares that people actually took home instead of selling the same day. When it is high, more genuine buyers or sellers are involved and fewer day-traders.

Fine-tune
How does it decide?
  • "Usual" is the average of the days right before that day. We need at least 5 earlier days, and 20 or more works best.
  • Each day is checked two ways: its delivery %, and the number of shares delivered. Each check earns 2 points if it is at least your "strong" level above usual (1.3 times by default), 1 point if it is at or above usual, and 0 if it is below.
  • 3 or 4 points is Strong, 2 is Medium, and 0 or 1 is Weak.
  • A high number only counts if it beats this stock's own habit. If a stock delivers around 59% almost every day, then 59% is just its usual, so it will show as Medium or Weak.
  • If very few shares traded that day (under 0.6 times usual), the verdict is lowered one step, because a high % on very few trades is not reliable.
  • The price direction tells you whether it was buying or selling.
  • Weekend dates are special sessions (like Diwali Muhurat trading) and usually look unusual, so take them lightly.
  • "Since the move began" starts from the date you pick. By default that is the day with the lowest price in your data.
Using it on your phone
  • Android (Chrome): open the website, tap the menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
  • After your first visit it also opens without internet. Your data stays on your device and is never uploaded.
